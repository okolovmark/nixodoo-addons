Press **Support** in the header:

* switch between *Bugs* and *Others*, the requests you are a UAT user of,
  and press one to open its task;
* *New Support Request*: type a title, pick a type and press *Create*;
  the new task opens;
* *View Closed* lists your requests in folded stages.

Your requests open and list whichever companies you selected in the
company switcher; there is no need to select the company of the project
to read them. Select the request's company to change it: outside the
companies you selected the form may look editable, but saving is refused.
There you can still send a message in its chatter and attach files inside
*Send message*; attaching with the chatter's paperclip is refused.
