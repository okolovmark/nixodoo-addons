AI Assistant

The AI Assistant: Claude answers an employee’s question from live Odoo data, which it looks up through read-only tools that run as that employee. Every internal user has it, in two places:

  • Ask AI, a button in the chatter of a record: answers about that record and the records linked to it, as internal notes on the record;
  • the AI Assistant app: conversations on a full page or in a quick panel from the systray, and AI reports.

It only reads: it creates, changes and deletes no record and never offers to. The one thing it saves is an AI report, the employee’s own page. It answers on Claude only, the models of claude_models in order (Sonnet 5.5, then Haiku 5.5), never on another model: when no Claude model answers, nobody does. It answers in the language of the question, gives times in the employee’s timezone and names the records its answer rests on.

Ask AI

Ask AI sits after Log note in the chatter of a saved record (an icon on a phone). It opens a dialog for a question of up to 2,000 characters. The question is posted at once as a note by the employee; a queue job writes the answer as a second note by AI Assistant, in reply to the question, opening with a line that quotes the asker and the start of the question: two answers on one record may arrive in either order. Both carry the Ask AI subtype, which is internal and followed by nobody: they notify nobody, and every internal user who can see the record sees them. The asker does not become a follower.

Ask AI answers about the record and the records linked to it; for reports, rankings and statistics over other records it points to the app. It is refused on a record the employee may not read, on one the AI deny list holds, on a Discuss channel (its notes would reach the members) and on the assistants’ own ai.* records. A missing, hidden or denied record gets the same message.

The answer is escaped, then only its marks are rendered (bold, emphasis, code, headings, lists, paragraphs); only URLs into this Odoo, at its own host or a path of the web client, become links. A question without an answer gets a note instead:

  • it needed more tool rounds or time than one answer has: narrow it (one record, a shorter period) and ask again;
  • no Claude model answered: the AI Assistant is not available right now.

The record’s earlier Ask AI questions, from any asker, go with a new question as its history (see How an answer is made), as long as the question note and the note of its latest answer are still in the chatter. Another employee’s earlier question is passed with their name, quoted as data, never as an instruction.

The asker rates an answer with a thumbs-up or thumbs-down reaction on the answer note, also on a record they may only read. Only the asker’s own reactions count; a thumbs-down wins over a thumbs-up.

The AI Assistant app

The AI Assistant app opens on Assistant, which lists the employee’s conversations, the most recently active first, with New chat and My reports; administrators also have Review and Configuration (see Administration). A conversation is named after its first question and kept until its owner deletes it, which deletes its questions and answers; the AI reports it saved stay in My reports. An employee sees only their own conversations.

The magic-wand icon in the systray opens a quick panel over the current screen and carries it: the window action’s model and, on a form, the record shown. The panel continues the employee’s latest conversation while it was asked from the same screen; otherwise the next question starts a new one. Open in the AI Assistant app moves to the full page. A question in the app may be about any data the employee can see: the screen is context, not a limit. While an answer is written the composer waits; each answer has Helpful and Not helpful buttons, and the same button again takes the rating back. A new answer an administrator sends shows in the conversation as an answer quoting the question it answers again (see To Review).

How an answer is made

readme/answer_flow.puml draws one question.

A question is an ai.assistant.exchange row, answered by one queue job on the channel root.ai_assistant. The row keeps the companies selected in the switcher when the question was asked, the current one first; the answer reads in those the employee still works in, or in their main company when none is left. When the job runs, the employee’s group and, for Ask AI, their access to the record and the deny list are checked again. A question refused then is closed as Unavailable without asking Claude: nothing is posted on the record, and an Ask AI asker alone gets a notification that they no longer have access to the AI Assistant or to the record; in the app the refusal shows in place of the answer.

The job sends Claude the system prompt (a constant of the module), the history, and a context block (the employee, the date and time in their timezone, their companies, the record or screen) before the question, then runs the tool loop of ai.claude.gateway._claude_chain (ai_claude). Each model has claude_max_tool_rounds (12) rounds of tool calls and claude_tool_budget_seconds (300) from its first call; a tool asked for past the budget is not run. A model that reaches either limit ends the question without an answer: the next model would meet the same limit.

The history is the last 10 answered questions asked before the question, oldest first: the record’s for Ask AI, the conversation’s in the app. Each goes with its latest answer: a new answer takes the place of the answer it answers again.

The job’s own transaction writes nothing; the answer is written in a short transaction of its own, only while the question is still pending, so a second run of the job never answers twice. That write is tried 3 times when it meets concurrent updates; then one more transaction closes the question as Unavailable, with the not available text, without asking Claude again. Should that meet a conflict too, the question stays pending for the cron below.

An answer that says neither the module documentation nor the data answers the question is flagged Not covered. A question whose job failed or was cancelled is closed at once as Unavailable, and the asker sees the not available text. The cron AI Assistant: close questions whose answer never came (every 5 minutes) closes the questions still pending pending_stale_seconds (1800) after their job started or was enqueued, or after the question when it has no job; a running job is never cut short. Whichever of these closes a question, an app asker who has lost the AI Assistant since sees the refusal instead; for a new answer the refusal is told to nobody.

Tools

The tools run as the asking employee, in the companies of the question, and refuse to run as the superuser: access rights, record rules, companies and field groups decide what they return, as they do on screen, then the AI deny list takes away what the assistant must not read. find_models reads the model and field lists (ir.model, ir.model.fields) as superuser, then keeps only the models the employee’s access rights let them read and the fields their field groups let them see, less the deny list. find_menus reads the menus, their actions, the access rights and the roles as superuser and returns only names from them; whether the employee can open a menu is decided as the employee. find_models, find_menus and module_docs read the installed modules behind a model as superuser too. Each call runs in a savepoint that is always rolled back, under a statement timeout of tool_seconds (15).

  • search_read: the records matching a domain with the fields asked for (none asked: the stored fields that are not binary, HTML or x2many), at most max_rows (200); it says when more match.

  • read: records by id, archived ones too, at most max_rows; it lists the ids that do not exist or that the employee may not see.

  • read_group: totals by group. Group keys are fields of the model itself, a date by day, week, month, quarter or year; measures are field:function or name:function(field) with sum, avg, min, max, count or count_distinct of a stored field; every group has __count; at most max_rows groups.

  • find_models: where a kind of data is kept, for a query of a few words, such as leave credit. The query is split at spaces, commas, semicolons and /, and each word loses the punctuation at its ends (an inner . or _ stays, as in hr.employee.credits). Single characters and common question words, such as how, many or left, are not looked for. no is looked for only right after a word that is, as in serial no, not in why is there no attendance. A note names the words not looked for (Not looked for: why, is, there, no.); of more than 10 words, only the first 10 are looked for, and a note says so.

    A model matches when its technical name, its description or the label of one of its fields holds a word of the query, ignoring case. A word of two letters, such as OT or SL, matches only a whole word; a longer one matches only where a word starts (after anything but a letter or a digit), so tag finds Tags but not Stage. A word of four letters or more that ends in s is also looked for as a singular: -ies becomes -y from five letters on, -sses, -xes, -ches and -shes lose es, any other -s but -ss loses its s. _ and % are plain characters. The fields every model has (id, create_uid, create_date, write_uid, write_date, display_name, __last_update) are never matched by label, and a model that only no matches is left out.

    It returns at most 15 models: those matching more of the words other than no first, then those whose name or description holds more of them as typed, then those whose name or description holds any of them, then those with a label holding no, then those with more matching fields, then by technical name. Each comes with its description, up to 10 matching fields (technical name and label, those matching more words first) with the count of the others, and the installed modules that define or extend it and have a README, which module_docs reads. Left out: models the employee’s access rights do not let them read, models on the AI deny list, transient and abstract models, and fields outside the employee’s field groups or on the deny list. A note also gives the count of matching models past 15, or, when none matches, suggests another word, a synonym or the singular.

  • find_menus: where a screen, menu or report is, for a query of a few words, such as inventory analysis. The query is read and its words match as for find_models, against the menu’s name, its action’s name and the names of the menus above it, in the employee’s language; a menu that only no matches is left out.

    It looks at the active menus whose action exists. Left out: menus without an action and menus under an archived one; developer mode menus (whose only group is base.group_no_one) and the menus Odoo keeps out of the employee’s web client whatever their access, with the menus under them.

    It returns at most 10 menus: those matching more of the words other than no first, then those matching more of them in their own or action name, then by path; a note gives the count past 10, or, when none matches, suggests another word, a synonym or the singular. Each comes with:

    • path: the menu names from the app down, joined by >;
    • model: the model its window, server, report or client action opens, when that model is installed; a URL action has none;
    • modules: as for find_models; or instead denied, when the model is on the AI deny list or a wizard’s, whose data and documentation the assistant does not read;
    • can_open: whether the employee can open the menu and every menu above it outside developer mode: their groups, and read access to the model of each window, server or report action on the path.

    A menu the employee cannot open also has needs, lists of group full names such as Invoicing / Billing: every list is required, one group of a list is enough. A list holds the groups of a menu on the path the employee has none of, or the groups whose access rights read the model of a window, server or report action on the path that the employee may not read; a list holding every group of another is left out. needs is absent when no group opens the menu, as when no access right lets anyone read such a model. With base_user_role installed, roles lists up to 5 roles, by name, that each grant a group of every list on their own, and more_roles counts the others.

  • fields: the fields the employee may see and the deny list leaves, with label, type, related model, help, selection values and whether they can be searched, sorted and grouped.

  • record_history: a record’s chatter, newest first, without user notifications: author, date, type, subtype, text (each cut at 2,000 characters), attachment names and tracked changes, at most max_rows messages, marked as untrusted data.

  • module_docs: the READMEs of the installed modules that define or extend a model, by name; a module may read others, or fewer, through ai.assistant.tools._module_docs_modules. Each README is the running module’s rendered one, as plain text, without its table of contents, changelog, bug tracker, credits, link targets and media.

  • report_run, report_save and report_get (app only): see AI reports.

A tool result holds at most 60,000 characters: search_read, read, read_group, find_models and find_menus drop rows from the end, fields drops fields and record_history the oldest messages, each with a note saying so. HTML comes as plain text, datetimes in UTC. module_docs shares one result among a model’s READMEs as evenly as their lengths allow; the module that defines the model, and those _docs_floor_module_names adds, keep at least a quarter each. A README cut to fit ends at a paragraph with [cut at X of its Y characters], a closing note names the modules cut or left out, and the tool with a module and an offset reads that README alone from X on.

The model reads errors as text written for it, never Odoo’s own message, which may name records read as superuser: a refused access is Not available: the employee may not read this, a query past the timeout asks to narrow it, an unexpected failure names only its type.

A model that is not installed is not a refusal: the error says it is not installed here and that the data may be kept in a model of a custom module, to look for with find_models. The system prompt says so too; a module may say more there through ai.assistant.engine._system_prompt_parts. An abstract model gets an error that it holds no records, also pointing to find_models. Both errors are logged as Error, not Denied.

The system prompt also tells the model never to guess a model: it finds one with find_models, or with find_menus when the question is where something is in Odoo, before it reads that model’s documentation or data.

Domains

A domain is a JSON array of [field, operator, value] terms and the prefix operators &, | and !. Every field of a path must be one the employee may read and the deny list leaves. Refused, with a reason the model can act on:

  • =?, and in or not in without a list (one id on a link is taken as a list of it);
  • a computed field Odoo cannot search;
  • child_of and parent_of on anything but ids, or on a link Odoo cannot walk. They take id or a stored, non-related link of the model’s own, or one inherited from a parent that stores it, to its own model or to a model whose hierarchy is stored or held by a plain link to itself; never messages. They start only from roots the employee may read and the deny list leaves, and Odoo resolves them with its own rules; a private address under a public contact never matches a child_of;
  • a path through a link to messages that Odoo does not join in SQL, or such a link compared with a name: Odoo would check every message one by one;
  • sorting by a contact or a copy of a contact’s data, grouping by a path, grouping or aggregating (other than counting) a copy of a contact’s data, and sorting, grouping or aggregating a message’s text, or a timesheet’s amount or margin, a sale margin or an analytic total where the rows hold or sum a timesheet;
  • reading a field by a dotted name, and reading a binary field.

Without an order, a search keeps the model’s own, less the terms that sort by a contact, then by id: users by login and id rather than by name, a contact’s; companies by sequence and id; contacts by id, the newest first.

One exception follows Odoo’s own search: on a path through an auto_join link (a record’s messages or activities, a user’s contact), and on a term that compares a link the deny list filters with ids, a range, a name search’s result or nothing, the linked rows are matched in SQL without their record rules, as Odoo joins them or compares a many2one’s column. The deny list’s row filter and the link’s own domain still apply, and every later step of the path is resolved with the employee’s rights and rules.

AI deny list

What the assistant never reads, even when the employee may: an Ask AI answer is seen by everyone who can see the record, not only by the asker.

  • Models (fnmatch patterns): hr.payslip*, hr.payroll.*, hr.contract*, hr.appraisal*, res.partner.bank, res.bank, ir.config_parameter, ir.mail_server, fetchmail.server, res.users.apikeys*, auth_totp.device, payment.provider, payment.token, mail.ice.server, mail.guest, sign.request.item.value, sign.log and ai.*; and every transient model, since settings and wizard rows hold what was typed in them.
  • Fields: the hourly cost and display_cost of a project’s employee mapping; account_number of bank statement lines; the task brief (brief of tasks, from project_ai_driven), the whole task in one text, its notes, their authors and its attachments included, past the masks applied value by value: the assistant reads a task field by field; can_open_last_appraisal on any model (hr_appraisal_oca’s flag on employees and their public profiles), a bit derived from the appraisals, a denied model; the website’s Plausible and Google Maps keys, a signature request’s share link, a Discuss channel’s uuid and invitation URL and a calendar event’s meeting URL (videocall_location), which ends with its access token; a mail’s headers (a digest’s unsubscribe link holds a token) and a link preview’s source_url, a message’s URLs kept outside its text; the costs of the Timesheet Attendance report (hr.timesheet.attendance.report: timesheets_cost, attendance_cost, cost_difference), hours times an employee’s hourly cost on every row, which names a user at most; and on every model, the fields named like a token, secret, password, API key or credential (access_token, signup_token, signup_url, invite_token, *_token, *secret*, *password*, *passwd*, *api_key*, credential*, callback_hash, visibility_password*).
  • Employees: every field of hr.employee that hr.employee.public does not have, but those ai.assistant.tools._employee_work_fields names, and every hr.employee field restricted to a group of hr or of an hr_* module.
  • Employee requests, the models ai.assistant.tools._request_models names (none here): readable, but none of their attachments, those posted in their chatter included, and none of their mails, which print the reason. Notes, tracked changes and comments without a file stay readable; their reason fields go on the deny list with them.
  • Private addresses: contacts of type Private, employees’ home addresses that are not a user’s own contact, and every contact under either. They are left out of every read; a link to one reads a private address, on the AI deny list, and so does a copy of its data: a related field through it, a stored text computed from it, a name taken from it, a message’s sender address, a mail’s recipients.
  • Timesheet costs: the amount of an analytic line that names an employee, its hours times the employee’s hourly cost, reads an employee’s cost, on the AI deny list and matches no filter, negated or not; it is never a sort key, group or total over rows that hold such a line. Analytic lines without an employee (accounting) keep their amount. A row of the Timesheets Analysis report (timesheets.analysis.report) that names an employee is masked the same, its amount and, with sale_timesheet, its margin, the revenue plus that cost. The totals of an analytic account with at least one line that names an employee, even a line the asker may not read, read the same: the account’s balance, debit and credit and a project’s analytic_account_balance, which beside the hours would give the rate. They are computed, so no filter, group or aggregate takes them, and a sort by one over such an account is refused. An account without such a line keeps its totals. With sale_timesheet and sale_margin, whose sale_timesheet_margin costs a sale line delivered by timesheets at their hourly cost, a sale line with a timesheet reads the same in its purchase_price, margin and margin_percent, and so do its order’s margin and margin_percent and the margin of that order’s Sales Analysis rows; there they match no filter, and a sort, group or total over such rows is refused. Only sale_margin’s fields are masked: a field of the same name that another module defines in its place, such as a margin over a bill of materials’ cost, reads as it is. The tracking values of every such cost or total stay out of the history, on whichever record they are logged; a change written into a message’s text instead, as tracking_manager logs a one2many line’s change on its owner, is not masked.
  • Links into a denied model, and related fields whose path crosses a denied field.
  • Rows of a denied record or a private address: its messages, mails, tracking values, notifications, reactions, followers, activities, attachments and calendar events. Also left out: the tracked changes of a denied field, of any field of a denied model on whichever record Odoo logs them (a bank account’s number on its holder’s chatter), or of a field the employee may not read, and attachments that hold a denied binary field or whose res_model names no model. A message loses its text, which then matches no filter, when it carries a refused attachment, when its body links to a record of a denied model (the note Odoo leaves on a contact when one of its bank accounts is deleted quotes the number) or when it holds a Discuss meeting’s URL (Contact Attendees sends it as a comment on the event); a link to a refused attachment reads an attachment on the AI deny list. In the text of the other messages and of mails, the value of each token in a URL (an access_token, token or other *_token parameter, the end of a Discuss meeting’s, a survey’s, a rating’s or a Discuss invitation’s URL, or of a URL whose path a module adds through _url_token_paths) reads [token-on-the-AI-deny-list], and a text filter matches the text as shown: a text that holds a token is matched with that placeholder in its place, whatever the token holds. No filter compares a message’s text with <, >, <= or >=, which would sort the texts, and none reads a mail’s own copy of its text (body_html and body_content of mail.mail), which would scan the whole HTML of every mail: the refusal points to body, the text of the mail’s message, or to the mail’s subject, recipients, state and dates.

deny_models_extra and deny_fields_extra add patterns, and so may a module through _deny_list_models and _deny_list_fields; narrowing the list is a code change. A refused call is logged as Denied, and the model is told the data is not available and not to reach it another way.

AI reports

In the app, a request for a report, a chart or a table to keep gets an AI report (ai.report). readme/ai_report_flow.puml draws its life. The model writes:

  • the specification: up to 8 datasets, each grouped (group keys and measures, as read_group) or a list of records (fields, as search_read) over a model and a domain, with an order and a limit; up to 20 formula columns per dataset or merge, over numbers, the columns before it, + - * / %, comparisons and abs, round, min, max and coalesce (parsed and walked, never run as code; a null operand or a division by zero gives null); and up to 4 merges of two grouped datasets on their group keys (inner, left or full), whose keys must be of the same kind;
  • the page: HTML and CSS with its written conclusions, and bindings that tie page elements, by id, to the data: single figures (the first, last, sum, average, minimum, maximum or count of a column, optionally of the rows matching a value), tables (columns, a limit, a total row) and Chart.js 2.9.4 charts (bar, horizontal bar, line, pie, doughnut, stacked or not).

report_run runs the specification as the employee through the same tools and returns all its data; past report_max_tokens (about two characters of JSON a token) it refuses and asks to aggregate further, as it does when a dataset without a limit matches more than max_rows. report_save runs it again and keeps the data with the page and bindings. The model types no figure into the page except in the conclusions, which quote what report_run returned. When a model saved a report and the next model starts over, that model’s first save replaces it. report_get returns a report the employee may open, as untrusted data, to save a revised version as a new report; a report a colleague shared comes without its specification.

The page keeps only its layout and text: headings, paragraphs, sections, lists, tables and inline formatting, <style> at the top of the page, and the attributes id, class, style, title, lang, dir, align, width, aria-* and, on cells, colspan and rowspan. Scripts, event handlers, images, SVG, forms and every link’s href are removed; a style sheet or style attribute whose CSS loads a file or a font or runs script (url(), image-set(), @import, @font-face, expression(), -moz-binding, behavior) is removed whole. A tag removed leaves its content in place; the cleaning runs again on its own output until its parser (libxml2) gives it back unchanged, three passes at most. A page that is mostly script is refused. A title has up to 120 characters, a page up to 100,000.

The app shows the page in a sandboxed frame that holds a second sandboxed frame with the page, both under a content security policy that refuses every request and lets only Chart.js and the module’s renderer (static/report_frame/report_renderer.js) run, by their hashes. The renderer fills the bindings from the stored data. A figure over a dataset whose limit left rows out is marked (partial), a table or chart over one says it holds the first rows, in the reader’s language: the server sends these notes translated with the data.

The data is a snapshot taken when the report was saved. Refresh, for the owner only, reruns the specification as the owner, without the model, in the report’s companies the owner still works in, the current one first; the page and its conclusions stay as written, and the report then says they were written on older data. A refresh that fails says why as the tools tell the model: their own reasons as written, a refused access, a timeout or an unexpected error by kind only (logged as a warning), never Odoo’s message. A model the report reads that is no longer installed is told in plain words: the model X it reads is not installed any more. A failed refresh keeps the report’s data. Export downloads an xlsx file: a sheet of the report’s facts, then a sheet per dataset and merge.

Share lets the owner choose colleagues who use the AI Assistant. They get the report itself, the owner’s snapshot: title, owner, dates, page and data, which they may export, but neither refresh nor share. The conversation and answer it came from, whom it is shared with, its specification and its companies are for administrators only, and nobody else filters, sorts or groups reports by them; the owner sees whom it is shared with in the app, and the AI Assistant reads the specification for them with report_get. Each share and unshare is logged (by whom, with whom, when, and the report’s title), and the log outlives the report. The owner and administrators may delete a report; it is then gone for everyone it was shared with.

Administration

For Settings administrators the app has two more menus. Each window is titled as its menu.

Review:

  • To Review: the answers to review, Ask AI and app alike (see To Review);
  • Conversations: the conversations of every assistant (filters Ask AI, AI Assistant): one Ask AI conversation per record, shared by its askers, and one per app conversation. Each shows its transcript, its answers with their cost and attempts, and a chatter of deviations: a fallback to the next model, a question no Claude model answered, a spend limit. Not helpful, the stored count of answers their askers rated down, is a column of the list, a figure on an AI Assistant conversation’s form and a filter; with With fallbacks also on, the list holds the conversations that are both;
  • Assistant Answers: every question with its kind (Ask AI or AI Assistant), employee, record or screen, companies, state (Pending, Answered, Unavailable), feedback, Not covered, review (Fixed, Dismissed), model, tokens, cost, latency, attempts and the AI reports it saved;
  • Tool Calls: every tool call with its employee, AI conversation, tool, model, arguments, rows returned, the modules whose README text module_docs returned, outcome (OK, Denied, Error), error and latency, never the data it read. A row is written in a transaction of its own, so it outlives a failed answer, and is deleted after 12 months;
  • Reports: every AI report, with its share log.

Configuration:

  • Settings: the parameters of the AI Assistant, but the runtime state (see Configuration).

A module of another assistant may add its own screens to these menus.

The cron AI Assistant: watch for Claude going quiet, every 15 minutes, runs the Claude incident check of ai_claude for the AI Assistant: when Claude answered none of at least 3 questions in 30 minutes, it mails the incident recipients, and again when Claude answers. A question that ran out of tool rounds or time counts for neither: Claude answered, the question needed more lookups. The AI Assistant has a Claude circuit breaker of its own.

To Review

An answer is to review while its asker rated it down, it is flagged Not covered or it was not answered (Unavailable), and no administrator has closed it. Review > To Review lists these answers, the newest first; Assistant Answers has the same filter, To Review, next to Fixed and Dismissed. An answer’s form shows To Review, Review, Reviewed by and Reviewed on, the answer it is a new answer to, and the new answers sent to it.

The review buttons are for Settings administrators. Fixed and Dismiss, on the form of an answer to review or on a selection in the list, close the selected answers still to review as Fixed or Dismissed, keeping who and when; the others are left as they are. Nothing reopens a closed answer.

Send new answer, on the form of an answer with a thumbs-down, Not covered or not answered, to review or closed, asks the AI Assistant the question again after a confirmation. It is refused with the reason, and nothing changes, while a new answer to the question is still being written, or when the asker is archived, no longer has the AI Assistant group, no longer has any of the companies the question was asked in or, for Ask AI, may no longer Ask AI on the record (it was deleted, they may no longer read it, or the AI deny list holds it). Otherwise the answer is marked Fixed if it is still to review (a Dismissed one stays so), and the new answer is queued to run as the asker: in their language and timezone, with their rights, on the same record or screen, in the companies of the question they still have, with the history from before the question.

The new answer replies to the question:

  • Ask AI: a note by AI Assistant in reply to the question note, opening with New answer to the question of, the asker and the start of the question;
  • app: an answer in the conversation, without the question repeated, opening with New answer to your question of, the date it was asked and the start of the question. The conversation’s transcript marks it New answer to the question of that date.

Once it is answered, the asker alone is notified, in their Inbox or by email: for Ask AI by the note itself, for the app by a note of AI Assistant on the conversation with the link Open it in the AI Assistant. The View button of that mail and its Inbox entry open the app on the conversation too; should its owner open the conversation’s form, it shows neither its figures nor its transcript and answers. A new answer that is not answered notifies nobody and shows as any unanswered question does; one refused, however it is closed, posts nothing and tells nobody. Either way it is to review.

A new answer is an answer like any other: it may be rated, reviewed and answered again; a new answer to a new answer quotes the question the asker asked. Later questions carry the new answer in their history, not the answer it replaced.

The cron AI Assistant: weekly digest of answers to review mails digest_recipients every answer to review, whatever its age, at most 50, the newest first. Those created in the last 7 days are marked New; the mail counts them, the answers to review and the questions asked in those 7 days, where a new answer an administrator sent never counts as a question asked. Each row gives the date, the asker, the kind (for a new answer, with the date of the question it answers), why it is to review, the record or screen, the modules whose documentation the answer read, links to the answer (Review) and to its AI conversation, then the question and the answer or the error. Nothing is sent while no answer is to review and no question was asked in the last 7 days; without recipients the digest is only logged.

A module of the organisation’s own

What an organisation changes is in methods its module overrides, calling super():

  • ai.assistant.tools: _deny_list_models and _deny_list_fields (with_patterns_after puts a pattern right after the shipped one it follows), _employee_work_fields, _request_models, _tool_texts (who keeps and documents the data, in the tools’ descriptions and errors), _model_modules (the modules whose READMEs may document a model), _module_docs_modules and _docs_floor_module_names;
  • ai.assistant.engine: _system_prompt_parts (the organisation, the paragraph on models that are not installed, whom to ask) and _default_claude_key_env (see Configuration);
  • ai.assistant.exchange: _incident_recipients.

The shipped words name no organisation: our Odoo, an administrator, Odoo, modules.

Installation

Installing needs ai_claude and the anthropic and httpx2 Python packages; give the job channel a capacity (see Configuration).

A database that holds the AI Assistant under another module

The AI Assistant may be installed already under another module, which then hands it over: that module drops it from its code and depends on this one, and its update installs this module first. Update it with the server stopped. ai_claude installs in the same update and adopts its own records first (see its README).

  • Before any data file is loaded, the pre_init_hook takes the records that module holds under this module’s external ids: the models, their fields and selection values (the Ask AI and AI Assistant values of the conversation’s Assistant, with their conversations), the group, access rights and record rules, settings, the Ask AI subtype, the AI Assistant partner, the job channel and job functions, the crons that close stale questions and send the weekly digest, the xlsx report, views, actions and menus, with the constraints and many2many tables of its models. Every record keeps its database id: conversations, answers, tool calls, AI reports, their shares and share logs stay. A holder is a module that holds the external id of a model of this module; one that held none of the records of its data files keeps its settings.
  • The holder’s settings, <holder>.<name>, become ai_assistant.<name> with their values, unless that key exists already. Each setting of this module’s data file whose key has a row takes its external id on that row, so a setting emptied and filled again keeps its value.
  • The data files loaded at install write even noupdate records: the post_init_hook writes back the values the adopted noupdate records had, so settings, crons and the job channel stay as they were, and removes again a forcecreate="0" setting the holder no longer had (an emptied one), which the data file creates at install.
  • The holder’s xlsx report model, report.<holder>.ai_report_xlsx, becomes report.ai_assistant.ai_report_xlsx, with its fields, under the same ids, unless that model exists already.
  • The cron AI Assistant: watch for Claude going quiet is this module’s own and is created at install.
  • A database where no other module holds a model of this module is left untouched, and a second run finds nothing to move.

Configuration

Internal User implies the hidden group AI Assistant (ai_assistant.group_ai_assistant): every internal user has the app, the systray panel and Ask AI. The app’s Review and Configuration menus, the Fixed, Dismiss and Send new answer buttons, every conversation and every AI report are for Settings administrators.

AI Assistant > Configuration > Settings opens the AI Assistant block of the Settings app, also listed there. Its section AI Assistant sets the parameters below, prefix ai_assistant.. A value in brackets is installed with the module only: an update neither brings back an emptied parameter nor adds one new to the data file, which takes a migration. An emptied text field or effort removes its parameter; its default then applies and the form shows it: the installed value, but nothing for claude_models and claude_key_env. A number must be above zero: the form refuses 0 or less, and an emptied number field is 0. A number parameter set in System Parameters to 0 or less, or to anything but a whole number (any number for tool_seconds), is read as its default, and a claude_effort the API does not accept as medium; the form shows what is read. Saving any Settings page writes the default shown for an absent parameter.

  • Claude: claude_models (claude-sonnet-5-5,claude-haiku-5-5), the Claude models, comma-separated, asked in order; empty, the AI Assistant does not answer. claude_key_env (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY), the name of the environment variable of the Odoo service that holds the Anthropic API key; the key is never stored in the database. While the variable is unset or empty, the AI Assistant does not answer. claude_effort (medium; low, medium, high, xhigh or max), claude_max_tokens (16000) and claude_timeout_seconds (120): per Claude call. claude_max_tokens caps the whole reply, an AI report’s page and bindings included.
  • Tools: claude_max_tool_rounds (12) and claude_tool_budget_seconds (300), one model’s tool loop; tool_seconds (15), the statement timeout of one tool call, fractions allowed; max_rows (200), the most rows or groups one tool call returns, and the most a report dataset holds.
  • Circuit breaker: circuit_threshold and circuit_cooldown_seconds, the AI Assistant’s Claude breaker, not installed; absent, 3 failed questions in a row and 300 seconds.
  • Answers and AI reports: pending_stale_seconds (1800), a question still pending this long is closed as Unavailable; report_max_tokens (200000), the most data, in estimated tokens, the report tools hand the model.
  • AI deny list: deny_models_extra and deny_fields_extra (empty), comma-separated fnmatch patterns added to the AI deny list, models as x_salary* and fields as model.field, such as sale.order.x_margin*. They only add to the list.
  • Notifications: incident_emails (empty), recipients of the mails on Claude not answering the AI Assistant, empty: nobody is mailed (ai.assistant.exchange._incident_recipients may name others); digest_recipients (empty), recipients of the weekly digest, empty: it is only written to the server log. Both comma-separated.

The breaker and incident state, claude_consecutive_failures, claude_circuit_open_until and claude_incident_open, is written by the module and is in Settings > Technical > Parameters > System Parameters (developer mode) only.

Set the variable claude_key_env names in the environment of the Odoo service to a key of a workspace of the Anthropic Console of its own. That workspace’s monthly spend limit caps what the AI Assistant costs; Odoo keeps no budget of its own. A module that installs after this one may name another variable in ai.assistant.engine._default_claude_key_env. Only ai.assistant.engine._seed_claude_key_env reads it, and sets the parameter to it while the parameter still holds ANTHROPIC_API_KEY: it takes effect only where that module calls _seed_claude_key_env, for example from a noupdate <function> of its data, which runs at its install only. Like ai_claude, this module needs the anthropic and httpx2 Python packages to install.

Answers run as queue jobs on the channel root.ai_assistant. Give it a capacity in the job runner’s channels line of odoo.conf, for example root.ai_assistant:2: it caps how many answers wait on Claude at once.

A neutralized database has claude_models, digest_recipients and incident_emails blanked, so a copy of production never calls Claude, whatever key its host’s environment holds, and mails nobody.

Usage

About one record. Open it, click Ask AI in its chatter, write the question and click Ask. The question shows in the chatter at once and the answer note follows when it is written, as a reply to the question that opens by quoting the asker and the question, so two answers on one record are not mixed up. React with a thumbs-up or thumbs-down on the answer to rate it. A later question on the same record sees the record’s last 10 answered Ask AI questions with their latest answers, so a follow-up may build on them. Everyone who can see the record sees both notes. If, when the answer is due, you no longer have access to the AI Assistant or to the record, nothing is posted: a notification tells you alone that the question was not answered.

About anything else. Open the AI Assistant app, or click the magic-wand icon in the systray to ask about the screen you are on without leaving it. Ask in any language. The AI Assistant reads what you could look up yourself in Odoo, less the AI deny list; when it says it cannot see something, the data is outside your access rights or on the deny list. Ask it where a screen, menu or report is: it finds the menu, says whether you can open it and, when you cannot, which groups or roles open it. If you lose the AI Assistant while a question waits, the refusal shows in place of its answer.

AI reports. In the app, ask for a report, a chart or a table to keep. The answer shows a card that opens the report; My reports lists your reports and those shared with you. To change one, ask for the change in its conversation: the AI Assistant saves a revised version as a new report. In a report, Refresh computes the figures again from today’s data (the written conclusions stay as they were; when a model the report reads is no longer installed, it says so and the report keeps its data), Share shows it to chosen colleagues, Export downloads its data as xlsx, and the bin deletes it, for everyone it is shared with.

A new answer to your question. When an administrator has the AI Assistant answer one of your questions again, you are notified in your Inbox or by email. On a record the new answer is a note in reply to your question, opening with New answer to the question of. In the app it shows in the conversation, opening with New answer to your question of and the question; Open it in the AI Assistant, the mail’s View button and the Inbox entry open the app on that conversation. Rate it as any answer.

Review (administrators). Review > To Review lists the answers that got a thumbs-down, were not covered or were not answered, until they are closed. Each is a candidate for a README fix, since module_docs serves the READMEs as deployed. Open one to read its question, answer, attempts and cost, with a link to its AI conversation. Click Fixed once the README or the code is corrected, or Dismiss when there is nothing to correct; both also work on a selection in the list. Once the fix is deployed, Send new answer asks the AI Assistant the question again as its asker, who is notified of the new answer; the answer is marked Fixed at the same time if it is still to review. The weekly digest mail lists every answer still to review, those of the last 7 days marked New, each with a Review link to it. Review > Assistant Answers holds every question (filters Not helpful, Not covered, Unavailable, Pending, To Review, Fixed, Dismissed); Review > Tool Calls (filters Denied, Errors) shows what each answer looked up. Configuration > Settings sets the AI Assistant.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Claude only: when no Claude model answers (an outage, a missing key, the workspace’s spend limit), the AI Assistant does not answer. There is no fallback to another model and no per-employee budget in Odoo, so one heavy user can spend the workspace’s monthly limit for everyone.
  • It reads and never acts: it changes no record and offers no actions.
  • An Ask AI answer is seen by everyone who can see the record. Text written into a record or an incoming email can steer an answer, but cannot make it change anything.
  • A shared AI report shows the owner’s data, which may be wider than what its recipients may read themselves.
  • An Ask AI question refused when its answer job runs (the asker left the AI Assistant group, or Ask AI is no longer available on the record) is closed as Unavailable without a note on the record: only the asker learns of it, from a notification shown while they have Odoo open.
  • An AI report page holds no images, SVG, links or fonts of its own, and its charts are those of Chart.js 2.9.4.
  • Two concurrent presses of Send new answer on one question queue one new answer, guarded by a row lock on the question and a check read past the transaction’s snapshot. The tests run in one transaction and do not exercise that race.
  • find_models and find_menus match a word only where a word starts: keeping does not find Timekeeping.
  • find_models and find_menus do not reduce a word of three letters: OTs does not find OT.
  • find_models and find_menus form singulars by spelling alone: cookies does not find Cookie, nor statuses or analyses Status or Analysis.
  • find_models never matches the fields every model has by label, also where a model labels one otherwise, such as create_date as Creation Date.
  • do is a common word find_models and find_menus leave out, also where it stands for a delivery order.
