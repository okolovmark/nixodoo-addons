# ai_claude and ai_assistant on 20.0 follow the Odoo 20 core where it differs from 16.0

Status: accepted (2026-11-24)

The 16.0 branch built the two modules first. Its record, `docs/adr/0018-the-ai-assistant-is-ai-assistant-on-ai-claude-made-an-organisation-s-own-by-a-module-that-extends-it.md` there, holds why the Claude layer and the employee assistant are two modules, how an organisation's own module extends them, and how they adopt the records another module holds. This one states where the 20.0 port departs from 16.0.

The port keeps what the modules do: the Claude chain with its tool loop, breaker, costs and incident check, the AI conversation, Ask AI in the chatter, the app with its systray panel, To Review and *Send new answer*, AI reports, the read-only tools that run as the employee, and the AI deny list. Odoo 20 changed what several of these rest on. Where it did, the modules follow Odoo 20.

**Fresh installs only.** On 16.0 a `pre_init_hook` of each module takes over the records a database holds under another module, and a `post_init_hook` of `ai_assistant` writes back the adopted noupdate records. No database that has to be kept is known to run this branch (ADR 0001), so none holds these records under another module. Both hooks and their tests are left out; the modules install from scratch.

**Settings are read and written as text.** Odoo 20's `ir.config_parameter` reads and writes through typed methods, and `get_int` and `get_float` log a warning on every malformed value. Every parameter is read with `get_str` and parsed by the module, as on 16.0: anything but a value above zero reads as the default. The breaker and incident state is written with `set_str`.

**Access is `ir.access`.** Odoo 20 holds access rights and record rules in one model, `ir.access`: a line names a group, its operations and a domain. The 16.0 rights and rules become the lines of `security/ir.access.csv` of each module, with the same reach. The group `ai_assistant.group_ai_assistant`, hidden on 16.0, is *User* of the privilege *AI Assistant*, and *Internal User* implies it through the group's `implied_by_ids`.

**Links are `/odoo` routes.** A record opens at `/odoo/<model>/<id>`: the incident mail's link to a conversation, the digest's links to an answer and its conversation. The app opens on a conversation at `/odoo/action-ai_assistant.app?conversation_id=<id>&menu_id=<menu>`, the link of a new answer's note and of a mail's *View* button for the conversation's owner. Answers keep linking only URLs at Odoo's own host and paths of the web client; their host is read with `urllib.parse.urlsplit`, and a URL whose host it refuses stays text.

**Domains and groups in Odoo 20's terms.** Domains are `Domain` objects, a path resolved one `any` per hop and a row filter matched past the linked rows' access with `any!`, as Odoo 20 does for a link with `bypass_search_access`, the field attribute that replaced `auto_join`. The tools group with `_read_group`. `ai.report` hides its sharing and source fields from filters, sorts, groups and aggregates in `_search`, `_read_group` and `_read_grouping_sets`: the web client's `formatted_read_grouping_sets` reaches the SQL through the last without the second.

**Tracked changes are read from the message body.** Odoo 20 writes a message's tracked changes into its body, each field named by its label, and keeps `mail.tracking.value` in the optional module `mail_tracking`. `record_history` parses the body. The deny list hides a change by label, in every installed language, as the sanitizer stores it: the label is serialised with lxml, quotes as they are. Hidden are the changes of a denied field, of a field the employee may not read, and of any field of a denied model. Such a field's label hides a change on the messages of its own model, of every model linked with it by a relational field either way that tracks no readable field of that label, and of no model: Odoo logs one record's changes on a record linked with it (`_track_record`, a bank account's on its holder). A VAT number goes by every country's label of it, as Odoo names it after the company's country. Odoo tracks a property as `<field>: <property>`: the properties of a hidden properties field, and any many2one property into a denied model, are hidden alike, by the definitions as they are now. A tracked link into a model whose names the deny list may mask, a many2one property's included, goes by its label the same way: its tracking message loses its text, and `record_history` shows the change's old and new values as *a name on the AI deny list*. With `mail_tracking` installed, its tracking values are filtered by field as well.

**A bank account's number in message text.** Odoo 20 logs a new bank account on its holder as a link to the account, its changes through `_track_record`, and no deletion, so no message quotes a deleted account's number. The invoice mail (`account.email_template_edi_invoice`) prints the number of the invoice's bank account as plain text. A message, posted or scheduled, that links a record of a denied model, or whose body holds the number, as stored or sanitized, of a bank account its own record links to by a stored many2one, loses its text, and so do the copies of its text.

**Link previews are one per URL.** Odoo 20 keeps one `mail.link.preview` per URL (`_unique_source_url`), tied to each message that holds it by `mail.message.link.preview`, and lets an ERP manager read every one. The tools keep a preview and its message rows only for the messages the employee may read and whose text they show, looked for among the newest `MAX_SEARCH_LIMIT` messages with a link; a result that left out a preview, or a row of one, past that cut says so. A preview's record name is its URL (`_rec_name = 'source_url'`), on the deny list: its display name, and that of a link to it, reads *a name on the AI deny list*, and a name filter on it is refused. A model whose name search (`_rec_name`, `_rec_names_search`) reaches a hidden field, as a contact's reaches `vat` when `vat` is hidden, refuses name filters the same way. Odoo 20 searches a display name through `_search_display_name`; for a model that overrides it, the tools read the fields `NAME_SEARCH_FIELDS` declares for the override, and refuse a name filter while one of them, in any row, is shown otherwise than stored, and always for a model the list does not declare.

**Live chat visitors are guests.** Odoo 20 makes a live chat visitor a `mail.guest`, on the deny list, and builds the display name of a Discuss channel member, a live chat history row (`im_livechat.channel.member.history`), a call session and a group chat named by its members from the guest's name. The tools mask a display name Odoo builds from a denied record, through the fields its display name depends on, and a name built from such a one in turn, and its copies read the name shown (below). Odoo 20 also writes a guest's name into a live chat's own name, and the live chat report copies it. A live chat's name is not built from the guest record, so it shows, and so do its copies.

**Copies of a masked name read the name shown.** Odoo 20 copies a record's display name into the rows around it: the `record_name` of its messages and mails, the `res_name` of its attachments, activities and ratings, a rating's `parent_res_name`, a tracked link's old and new values in `mail_tracking`, and the texts of its messages, mails and scheduled messages (a reply's *Re: <name>*, an assignment notice, a mail's layout header, an origin link). Where the deny list masks the name, each copy, and a field related to one, reads the name the tools show for the record now. In a subject or body, each occurrence of the current hidden name of the message's record, of each record that one links to by a stored many2one, and of each record the text links (`data-oe-model`) reads the name shown, where a word starts, in any case, across any white space, as stored, HTML-escaped or token-redacted; a subject is never replaced whole. Odoo 20's reply-to names the author, not the record, and is shown as stored. A filter on a copy or such a text never finds, nor under a negation, a row whose value the masking changes or may change, decided in SQL from the rows' columns, without computing a display name: rows on a record of a denied model, of a model whose names are hidden or of one named by such a copy (a message, a rating), rows on a record whose name is built from a denied value or record where that source is set, following links, copies that differ from their record's stored name, texts whose record links a record whose name may be masked, texts whose own HTML links a record of such a model or of a denied one, and every tracked property link. A sort, group or aggregate other than a count by one is refused where a row the call reads may show it otherwise.

**A project update's description is masked as an employee's cost.** Odoo 20 writes no costs into an update's default text, but a database migrated from 16.0 keeps the costs, revenue and margin 16.0's `sale_timesheet` wrote there, and people type figures. The description goes by the employee-cost rule, as on 16.0, through the project's analytic account (`project_id.account_id.line_ids.employee_id`).

**Structured data is read, not compared.** Odoo 20 keeps more data as JSON: a contact's `additional_identifiers` and the identifiers metadata a localization computes from the VAT number (`l10n_nl`), beside properties. Its search compares a JSON or properties value as stored, URL tokens included. The tools show every text such a value holds with its URL tokens redacted, and refuse the field as a filter, a sort key, a group or an aggregate other than a count; a JSON or properties definition value may be tested set or not. An analytic distribution is filtered as Odoo 20 filters it, by its accounts (`distribution_analytic_account_ids`).

**Message searches page past Odoo's cap.** A search of `mail.message` that names no model, or more than five, is checked by Odoo 20 message by message: given a limit, it resolves the whole domain as superuser and keeps the readable rows of each batch; without one it checks every match at once and fails past `MAX_SEARCH_LIMIT`. The tools search such messages without a limit through `_search` past access, in the order asked (the model's own, newest first, by default, and `id desc` against ties), `IN_MAX` rows at a time, each batch searched again as the employee with the same domain, up to `MAX_SEARCH_LIMIT` matches. A refusal of a sort, group or aggregate, by an employee's cost or by a copy or text of a masked name, is decided from the rows the call reads: the employee's read access first, then the employee's own search among the rows that may show the value. `read_group` scans such messages before its refusals; `search_read` refuses every sort by a copy or text of a name on a scan that passes `MAX_SEARCH_LIMIT`, whatever the rows, as there the messages the employee may not read decide which ones the sort lets in.

**A one2many with a computed inverse goes through the stored links.** Odoo 20 computes the inverse of an analytic account's lines from the analytic plan in the context, so without that context the one2many finds no rows. Where a path or an employee-cost mask goes through a one2many whose inverse is neither stored nor searchable in SQL, the tools match the rows through every stored many2one of the comodel to the record's model, whatever the plan. Comparing such a one2many as a whole is refused.

**No private contacts.** Odoo 17 removed the *Private* contact type; an employee's private address is a set of fields of `hr.version`. The 16.0 machinery that left private addresses and every copy of their data out of every read is gone. `hr.version` is on the deny list, a field `hr.employee` shows from it goes by the employee rule, and a user's field that shows the namesake field of their employee goes with it.

**The deny list covers what Odoo 17 to 20 added.** Rows that belong to a record as its chatter does: scheduled messages and the files Odoo keeps under them until posting, and message translations, whose text is masked and redacted as a posted message's is; link previews go by the messages shown (above). Old versions of an HTML text, kept as diffs (`html_field_history`). Secrets: an automation rule's webhook UUID and URL, a server action's webhook URL, push devices and push queues, certificates and keys, the attendance kiosk's key and URL, a shared dashboard's URL and a product feed's URL. Employees' costs that sale margins carry: with `sale_timesheet`, a timesheet-delivered sale line's `purchase_price`, `margin` and `margin_percent`, its order's `margin`, `margin_percent` and `extra_total_fields`, and the *Sales Analysis* row of such an order; a project's estimate from `sale_project_margin`; the costs of the timesheet and attendance report. The VAT number's copies go with it: `account_edi_ubl_cii`'s `routing_endpoint` and `routing_identifier`, and every text or JSON field a model computes from its own `vat`, but the display name and the kinds of identification number (`VAT_FREE_FIELDS`).

**The client is Owl 3, its tests Hoot.** The 16.0 client patches the legacy messaging models and the chatter topbar; Odoo 20 has neither. *Ask AI* is a `patch(Chatter.prototype)` of `@mail/chatter/web_portal_project/chatter` with an extension of `mail.Chatter` that puts the button after *Log note*. Answers arrive over the bus through `usePlugin(BusPlugin)`, and a chatter showing the record fetches its new messages. A notification of a new answer opens the conversation in the app through `patch(Thread.prototype).open` for `ai.conversation`. The components use Owl 3 (`proxy`, `signal.ref`, `useProps`, `t-out`) and the `oi` icons; the panel is a service with a main component, the app a client action on `standardActionServiceProps` and `Layout`. The client's tests are Hoot files in `web.assets_unit_tests`, run in a browser, desktop and mobile, by HttpCase runners of the module (`TestHootDesktop`, `TestHootMobile`), since web's own suites run only in a run that loads web's tests; the tours use Odoo 20's syntax and `/odoo` URLs, two of them on a phone.

**Chart.js 4 in the report frame.** The frame inlines Odoo 20's Chart.js 4, allowed by its hash; the renderer draws a horizontal bar chart with `indexAxis: "y"`.

**Consequences.**

- A database that holds these records under another module is not taken over: on this branch the modules install fresh, and a holder on 20.0 would take a hook of its own.
- Tracked changes are told apart by label wherever `mail_tracking` is not installed: a hidden field's change logged on another record shows there when that record's model tracks a readable field of the same label or has no relational field to or from the hidden field's model, and a readable field's change is left out where a hidden field of its model shares its label. With `mail_tracking`, its tracking values are filtered exactly, by field.
- A message is masked by the bank accounts its record links to when the tools run: a mail sent before the record's account, or the account's number, changed keeps its text.
- Link previews are shown for the newest 10,000 messages with a link only.
- A message search that names no model, or more than five, looks at the first 10,000 matches: `search_read` says so, and `read_group` refuses past them, both asking to name the model.
- A live chat's own name, written from its visitor's, shows, and so do its copies and the live chat report.
- The copies and texts of a masked name are read for the current name of the record their row names and of its direct links only, a design this record accepts with its limits (`ai_assistant/readme/ROADMAP.rst`): a former name or link, a name reached through lines, a translation's or an SMS's text and a file's own name show as stored, and a filter on a copy or text leaves out more rows than the display changes.
- A JSON or properties field is read, not compared: no filter on its content, sort key or group; an analytic distribution is filtered by its accounts.
- The deny list holds no contact as such: a database upgraded from 16.0 whose former private contacts survive the upgrade reads them as any contact.
- The client's tests run wherever the module is installed or updated with tests, CI included.

Reversal cost: none of these is a choice between equals. Each follows how Odoo 20 stores, checks or renders the data, and undoing one sets the modules apart from the core they read through.
