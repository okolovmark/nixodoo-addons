A team's work in Project at a glance: a **Team Dashboard** of workload
cards per member, a multi-project **Gantt chart** with dependency lags and
baselines, a **Backlog** list, and planned dates of a task that roll up
from its sub-tasks. It builds on ``project_ai_driven`` (task type,
estimate, size, modules of a task, stage log) and on ``hr_timesheet``
(logged hours, employees).

Stages (``project.task.type``) gain two flags: **In Progress Stage**
(tasks there are being worked on; the dashboard picks a member's current
task from them) and **Backlog Stage** (tasks there form the backlog).

**Planned dates and sub-tasks**

A task has a planned start and a planned end (``planned_date_start``,
``planned_date_end``), on the task form and, hidden by default, in the task
list. A task without sub-tasks keeps the planned dates it is given, and
none until it is given some: assigning or closing the task sets none of
them. An end before the start is refused. These are the fields of the OCA
module ``project_timeline``, which this module does not need. With it
installed, its computations are replaced by these, so its rules that take
the assignment date as planned start and the closing date as planned end
do not run (``project_team_dashboard_timeline`` keeps its fields on the
form and list).

A task with active sub-tasks takes its planned start and end from them: the
earliest and the latest planned date of those sub-tasks, or no dates while
none of them is dated. Archived sub-tasks are ignored, the deadline is
never touched, and the roll-up climbs every level (Milestone > Task >
Sub-task). Such a task is flagged ``is_planned_from_subtasks``: its planned
dates are read-only on the task form and list, and a write that changes
them is refused with a message to change the sub-tasks instead.

**Backlog**

**Project > Backlog** lists the tasks in a Backlog stage with Key, Task,
Assignee, Type, Priority, Size and Estimate (h), filters *My Tasks*,
*Unassigned*, *Blocked*, *Overdue* and groupings by Assignee, Start Date,
Project and Type. Project users also search it by **Module**, a module's
technical name or title, as in the task search of ``project_ai_driven``.
It opens filtered on the user's **Default Dashboard Project** and on
unassigned tasks. **Estimate (h)** is the task's **Estimate** in hours,
and **Size** the size ``project_ai_driven`` computes from it.

**Team Dashboard**

**Project > Team Dashboard** is a client action with three modes:

* **View by Project**: one card per user assigned to an open task of the
  project (plus an *Unassigned* card).
* **View by Manager**: cards for the subordinates of a user, taken from the
  employee hierarchy down to a chosen depth (1 to 5 levels or all), across
  every project.
* **View by Department**: cards for the users of a department.

Each card shows the member's current task (the most recently updated task in
an In Progress stage), the queue of other in-progress tasks, the backlog,
other open tasks, open task count, estimated and remaining hours, hours logged
today and in the selected period (This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last
Month or a custom range), and an estimated completion date. The estimate
plans the remaining hours on the employee's working schedule with leaves
taken into account, and falls back to 5.7 hours a day when no employee or
schedule exists. Links open the member's kanban, backlog or closed tasks.

Users carry a **Default Dashboard Project**, editable from their
preferences.

**Gantt Chart**

The **Gantt Chart** view plots the planned dates of the selected projects'
tasks on a timeline: Day, Week or Month scale paged a month at a time (a
week on Day, a quarter on Month), grouped by user (the default), by task
or by stage, optional completed tasks, a counter of tasks without dates,
faceted search with a jump to any shown task by key or name,
several projects at once. Bars take the colour of their stage (closed
stages grey, legend built from the stages shown); an open task past its
planned end gets a red outline. Dependencies are drawn as orange elbow
connectors from the predecessor's end into the successor's start.
**Export Excel** downloads the open tasks of the selected projects that
have planned dates or a deadline as an .xlsx Gantt chart, sorted by start
date; closed tasks are always left out, and the search facets and grouping
are not applied. The chart is drawn with the vis-timeline library, which
the module ships (``static/lib/vis-timeline``, MIT License).

* **Attribute columns**: the left pane can show Assignees, Deadline, Estimate,
  Spent hours and Stage next to the task name, as a fixed-width grid
  with a header row; hours are right-aligned. The choice is stored per user
  (``res.users.gantt_columns``); the first four are on by default. Columns
  are shown only while their total width plus 140 px for the task name fits
  in 60% of the chart width (all five need a chart about 1010 px wide);
  otherwise they are hidden. The pane re-measures after a window resize or a
  column change. A cell tooltip reads "<Column>: <value>"; a header tooltip
  explains the column. The column that repeats the grouping is left out:
  Assignees in Group by User, Stage in Group by Stage; its checkbox is greyed
  out there but keeps the stored choice.
* **Dependencies**: dragging the handle of a task bar onto another bar makes
  the second task depend on the first (core ``depend_on_ids``). Clicking an
  arrow opens a popover to set its dependency lag or remove the link.
* **Dependency lag** (``project.task.dependency.lag``): calendar days per
  dependency, zero or more; no row means lag 0. The chart only checks it: a
  link whose successor starts before the predecessor's end plus the lag is
  drawn red with a warning. No date moves automatically. Copying a task
  keeps the lags of the dependencies that are copied with it; removing a
  dependency removes its lag.
* **Baseline** (``project.baseline`` / ``project.baseline.line``): *Save
  Baseline* (Project Managers) stores the planned start, planned end and
  deadline of every open task with at least one of these dates, one
  baseline per project shown. The new baseline becomes the project's
  **Active Baseline** (``baseline_id``) and replaces the previous one.
  *Show Baseline* draws it as grey bars under the tasks, and the tooltip
  shows the shift of the planned end, e.g. "+3 d vs baseline". Turning
  *Show Baseline* on when no task shown has a baseline raises a warning
  pointing to *Save Baseline*.
* **Sub-tasks**: grouped by task, user or stage, a sub-task row is nested
  under its parent's row when both fall in the same group. Otherwise the
  sub-task is a top-level row labelled "sub-task of <parent key>" (no label
  when the user cannot read the parent). A parent's bar is read-only when its
  dates come from its sub-tasks; other bars move and resize by whole days. In
  Group by User a task and its own sub-tasks never count as conflicting.
* **Unscheduled (N)**: tasks with no planned dates and no deadline are
  listed in their own group at the end of the chart; a click opens the
  task.
* **Milestones**: Milestone-type tasks and tasks with only a deadline are
  drawn as a diamond on their date, and exported as milestones in the
  Excel Gantt chart. A Milestone shown with its sub-task rows and spanning
  more than one date is drawn as a bar.
* **Stage History** (off by default, in every grouping): each task
  row expands into one row per stage visit, in the stage's colour and
  chained by orange connectors. The visits come from the stage log of
  ``project_ai_driven`` (``project.task.stage.log``, *The stage log* in
  that module), which holds every change of a task's stage, with or
  without a line in the chatter, and are shown for the tasks the user can
  see. A row with a stage starts a visit at its date and ends the visit
  before it; a row with no stage only ends the visit before it. The
  history starts at the task's first row. The visit a task is still in
  runs to now, or ends at its own start when its stage is closed (folded).
  A deleted stage keeps its name and shares the colour and the legend
  entry of tasks without a stage. A visit shorter than a day is drawn one
  day wide; history rows are read-only. The task row then shows a
  read-only summary bar from the start of the first visit to the end of
  the last one, in the current stage's colour (the planned dates stay in
  its tooltip), and the User grouping's conflict highlighting is off. A
  task with no row in the log keeps its own bar.

Lags and baselines are read and written only through the Gantt methods,
which check the user's rights on the tasks and projects first.
``readme/gantt_data_model.puml`` draws the models of the Gantt chart; the
design record is
``docs/adr/0015-the-team-dashboard-needs-no-project-timeline-and-adopts-its-records-by-external-id.md``
in this repository.

Installation
============

A database that has none of this module's records needs nothing beyond
installing it. The install creates the planned date columns of the task
when ``project_timeline`` has not created them, empty, and rolls the
planned dates of every task with sub-tasks up from them; it logs ``Planned
dates rolled up from sub-tasks on <n> tasks``.

**A database that holds these records under another module**

The Backlog, the Team Dashboard and the Gantt chart may be installed
already under another module, which then hands them over: that module drops
them from its code and depends on this one, and its update installs this
module first.

* Update that module with the server stopped. Before any data file is
  loaded, the ``pre_init_hook`` takes the records that module holds under
  the external ids of this module (models, fields, views, actions, menus,
  access and record rules, the constraint of the dependency lag), with the
  constraints and foreign keys registered for them, so every record keeps
  its database id: baselines, dependency lags, the stage flags and the
  default dashboard project of every user stay. The module is the one that
  holds the external id of ``project.baseline``.
* The same hook hands the two views of that module that extend the planned
  dates of ``project_timeline`` to ``project_team_dashboard_timeline``,
  which installs itself in the same update and loads its views over them.
  Put both modules on the addons path: without the glue module, those two
  views stay as they are and the form shows the planned dates twice.
* A database where no other module holds the external id of
  ``project.baseline``, ``project.baseline.line`` or
  ``project.task.dependency.lag`` is left untouched, even where a module
  holds records of the same names, and a second run finds nothing to move.

Configuration
=============

Projects
~~~~~~~~

* Tick **Task Dependencies** on the project to link its tasks from the
  Gantt chart.
* On the project's task stages (Project > Configuration > Task Stages,
  developer mode only) tick **In Progress Stage** on the working stages and
  **Backlog Stage** on the backlog stages; the Backlog list and the Team
  Dashboard read these flags.
* Tick **AI-driven Project** on a project for the Deadline of its tasks to
  follow their planned end (see Usage). Ticking it changes no Deadline:
  each task follows from the next change of its planned end. Installing
  the module, or updating it to 16.0.1.1.0, sets the Deadline of the open
  tasks (stage not folded, not archived) of AI-driven projects that have a
  planned end to the day of that end, without changing their last-updated
  date, in the time zone of the first active assignee who has a valid one,
  else of the user who last changed the task if still active, else UTC.
  Closed and archived tasks keep their Deadline until their planned end
  changes.

Users
~~~~~

* Each user sets their **Default Dashboard Project** in Preferences (an
  administrator on the user form); the Backlog menu opens on it.
* The estimated completion of a member card plans on the working schedule
  of the user's employee in the current company, or of the company: give
  each member an employee with a schedule for an estimate that skips
  weekends, leaves and public holidays.

Access
~~~~~~

Team Dashboard tasks are read with the user's project rights, and so are the
logged hours in View by Project mode. In the Manager and Department modes the
logged hours of the listed users (across all projects), the employee hierarchy
and the department members are read with elevated rights; the department
picker list and the viewer's own department are loaded with the user's own
rights.

Who opens which team in those two modes follows the user's employee:

* **View by Manager**: the user themselves, or a manager one of whose
  employees reports to one of the user's employees, directly or further down
  the hierarchy (the employee's *Manager*), in any company.
* **View by Department**: a department the user's employee belongs to, or
  one it is the *Manager* of.
* A user with **Timesheets: User: all timesheets** (Administrator includes
  it) or **Employees: Officer** opens every manager and every department.
  So does every **Project / Administrator**: Odoo gives project
  administrators the first of these rights.

A manager's team is everyone below their employees, the manager left out,
also where the hierarchy loops back: Odoo lets an employee manage their own
manager, and the *Manager* of a department who belongs to it gets themselves
as *Manager*.

The pickers still list every user and department. Any other choice is
refused: the user gets an error message, and the picker goes back to the
manager or department shown before, whose cards stay on screen. Give a team
lead an employee with the right *Manager* for them to see their team.

Gantt chart: dependencies can be linked and lags edited by users with write
access to the dependent task. Only **Project / Administrator** can save a
baseline, and needs write access to the project. Project managers can read
dependency lags and manage baselines directly; project users reach them only
through the Gantt chart.

Usage
=====

Plan a task
~~~~~~~~~~~

* The planned dates of a task are above its deadline on the task form;
  show them in the task list from its optional columns.
* A task with active sub-tasks shows its planned dates read-only: plan its
  sub-tasks and the task follows them.
* In an AI-driven project the **Deadline** follows the planned end: when a
  task gets a planned end or its planned end really changes, by hand, in
  the Gantt chart or from its sub-tasks, its Deadline becomes the day of
  that end in your time zone (UTC when your preferences have none). A task
  moved into an AI-driven project with a planned end gets that Deadline
  too. Nothing else moves the Deadline: not a stage change, not a start
  moved alone in the Gantt chart, not a change to a sub-task that leaves
  its parent's planned end where it was, not the same planned end saved
  again. A Deadline you set by hand, alone or together with the planned
  end, stays until the planned end changes again. On the task form the
  Deadline changes as you edit the planned end; a Deadline you type after
  it is the one saved. Changing the Deadline alone leaves the plan where
  it is, and clearing the planned end leaves the Deadline. In other
  projects the planned end does not change the Deadline.

Work the backlog
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Project > Backlog** opens the Backlog-stage tasks of your Default
Dashboard Project, filtered on *Unassigned*; remove that filter to see
assigned tasks too. Reorder by drag, filter *My Tasks*, *Blocked* or
*Overdue*, and group by Assignee, Start Date, Project or Type. To assign a
task, open it.

Team Dashboard
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Project > Team Dashboard**:

#. Pick the mode: **View by Project** (type a project name), **View by
   Manager** (pick the manager and the **Depth**) or **View by Department**.
#. Pick the **Period** for the logged-hours figures, or *Custom Range* with
   start and end dates.
#. Read each member card: current task, queue, backlog, estimated and
   remaining hours, hours logged, estimated completion. Click a task key to
   open it, or the card links to the member's kanban, backlog or closed
   tasks.
#. Switch to **Gantt Chart** to see planned dates. Change the **Scale**
   (Day, Week, Month), page earlier or later with the arrows around
   **Today**, **Group** by user (the default), task or stage, press
   **Show Closed** to include completed/closed tasks, add more projects with
   the *Add project...* search, and press **Export Excel** for an .xlsx (the export
   always leaves out closed tasks).

Plan in the Gantt chart
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Columns: open the cog menu and tick the attribute columns to show; hover
  a column name there or in the header for what it holds. On a narrow
  screen the columns are hidden until the window is wide enough for them,
  or until fewer are ticked. Grouped by User or Stage, the column with the
  same information (Assignees or Stage) is not shown.
* Find a task: type its key or part of its name in the search box and pick
  it under *Go to task* (or press Enter for the first one); the chart
  scrolls to its bar, selects it and briefly highlights its row.
* Link tasks: hover the predecessor's bar, drag the handle at its right end
  and drop it on the dependent task. Click the arrow to enter **Lag, days** and press
  *Save*, or press *Remove* to delete the dependency. A red arrow means the
  dependent task starts too early; move its dates yourself.
* Project Managers press **Save Baseline** to store the current plan; it
  replaces the project's previous baseline. Press **Show Baseline** to
  compare the plan against it: the baseline is drawn as a thin bar under
  each task bar. When none of the tasks shown has a baseline yet, a warning
  says so and points to Save Baseline.
* Move or resize a bar to replan a task; it moves by whole days. The bar
  of a task planned from its sub-tasks does not move: drag its sub-tasks.
  A move changes only the planned dates the task has (a milestone's start
  and end together); drag the padded edge of a one-day bar to give the
  task its missing date.
* Press **Stage History** to see under each task the stages it went
  through and how long it stayed in each. Every stage change counts,
  whoever made it: a move by a script or an assistant that left no line in
  the chatter shows too. A task older than the stage log with no stage
  change in its chatter shows its current stage alone, from its last stage
  update.
* Expand **Unscheduled (N)** to find tasks without any date and open
  them to plan them.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

* The Gantt chart does not reschedule tasks: a violated dependency lag is
  only drawn red.
* Dependency lags count calendar days, not working days.
* Each project keeps a single baseline; saving a new one deletes the
  previous one, so there is no baseline history.
* The Gantt attribute columns cannot be sorted.
